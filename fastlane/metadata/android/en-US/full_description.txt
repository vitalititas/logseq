Asymmetric AI is a privacy-first knowledge workspace for notes, research, tasks, and connected thinking. It focuses on local control, longevity, and fast capture, with support for Markdown, Org-mode, PDF annotation, and structured workflows for organizing ideas.

The Whiteboard feature lets you organize knowledge and ideas using a spatial canvas with shapes, drawings, website embeds, and connectors. You can visually group and link notes and external media, giving visual thinkers a practical way to compose, remix, annotate, and connect material from their knowledge base.

In addition to its core features, Asymmetric AI keeps the plugin and theme workflow surface available for customization. Whether you're studying, researching, building, or managing day-to-day notes, it is designed for a clear and organized approach to working with your own knowledge base.
